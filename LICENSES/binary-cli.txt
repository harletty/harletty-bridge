What it is made of:

- The harletty CLI and most of the crates it is built from are Apache-2.0
  (LICENSE.Apache-2.0).
- It statically links the `dca` crate, which is LGPL-2.1-or-later
  (LICENSE.LGPL-2.1) because it is based on FFmpeg's DCA decoder. Its
  copyright holders are listed in NOTICE.dca.

How the binary is distributed:

Apache-2.0 is not compatible with version 2.1 of the LGPL, so for this
combined binary `dca` is used under the "or any later version" option of
its licence, as LGPL-3.0. The binary as a whole is conveyed under
LGPL-3.0: the Apache-2.0 parts remain available under Apache-2.0 on their
own, and Apache-2.0 code may be combined into a work under version 3 of
the GNU licences.

The complete corresponding source of this binary, which lets you rebuild
it with a modified `dca`, is the harletty-bridge repository at the tag of
this release: https://github.com/harletty/harletty-bridge
